# Shooting stars

Objective: occasional realistic meteors in index.html.
- Random interval, roughly one per 1-3 minutes.
- Fast streak (real meteor speed: crosses part of the sky in well under a second) with a fading tail that lingers briefly.
- Random position/direction; render between stars and aurora or above both, whichever looks better.
Non-goals: no meteor showers/radiant simulation.

## Acceptance Criteria

Meteors appear occasionally, look like quick bright streaks with fading tails, do not disturb the calm feel.
